# Dietary Needs (LLM Instructions) 
## Vegetarian
Exclude: Meat, poultry, fish and seafood, animal-derived broths or stocks, gelatin, animal fats

## Vegan
Exclude: Meat, poultry, fish and seafood, dairy, eggs, honey, animal-based broths or stocks, gelatin, animal fats

## Pescatarian
Exclude: Meat, poultry, animal-based broths from meat or poultry, gelatin

## Dairy-Free
Exclude: All dairy products including milk, cheese, butter, ghee, yogurt, kefir, cream-based sauces, whey, casein, condensed milk, evaporated milk, and milk powder

## Gluten-Free
Exclude: Wheat and wheat-based products, barley, rye, seitan, regular soy sauce, malt vinegar, malted products

## Nut-Free
Exclude: Tree nuts, peanuts, nut butters and spreads, nut milks, nut-based flours

## Soy-Free
Exclude: Soybeans, soy milk, soy sauce, tamari, miso, tofu, tempeh, soy protein isolate

## Egg-Free
Exclude: Eggs and egg-based products (including mayonnaise, egg noodles, baked goods with egg, and sauces containing egg)

## Shellfish-Free
Exclude: Shrimp, crab, lobster, crayfish, clams, mussels, scallops, oysters, squid, octopus, and sauces made with shellfish

## Keto
Exclude/Minimize: Grains and starches, sugars and sweeteners, high-carb fruits, legumes, starchy vegetables, low-fat or fat-free products

## Low-Carb and Paleo
Instruction: No special exclusions beyond general preference.
